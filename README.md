# Old Photo Restoration - Before & After Compare Tool

A lightweight, pure front-end web tool for comparing old photos with their restored versions. Drag the vertical divider to reveal the restoration results.

## ✨ Features

- **Drag to Compare**: Move the vertical divider left and right to see the original photo on the left and the restored photo on the right.
- **Alignment Fine-tuning**: Use the alignment panel to adjust the restored image (move up/down/left/right, zoom in/out) to perfectly match the original.
- **Multi-language Support**: Switch between Chinese and English with one click.
- **Copy Prompts**: One-click copy of professional restoration prompts (Chinese & English) for AI tools like Doubao.
- **Download Comparison**: Export the current comparison view as a high-quality JPEG image.
- **5 Demo Pairs Included**: Comes with 5 pairs of demo images (`imgbefore1-5.png` and `imgafter1-5.png`).
- **Drag & Drop Upload**: Support dragging and dropping your own images directly onto the canvas.
